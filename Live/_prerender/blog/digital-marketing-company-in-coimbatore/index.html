1. What is Digital marketing?

Digital marketing is all about using the power of the internet and digital technologies to promote your products and services. This involves leveraging various online channels like social media, email, search engines, and websites to reach potential customers.

2. Why Digital Marketing Matters:

Reach a Larger Audience: With billions of people online, digital marketing opens up opportunities to connect with a global audience, making it easier to grow your brand beyond borders.

Cost-effective: Compared to traditional marketing methods like print or TV ads, digital marketing is often much more affordable, allowing you to get the most out of your budget.

Trackable Results: One of the major benefits of digital marketing is that it lets you monitor and measure performance in real time. This means you can quickly assess what's working and make adjustments to optimize your strategy.
