Instagram: This platform is all about visuals! High-quality photos, reels, and influencer partnerships can help your brand gain attention. Using Instagram stories and interactive stickers (like polls and questions) can also boost engagement.

Twitter: Best for real-time updates and trending topics. Jumping into relevant conversations, using the right hashtags, and keeping tweets short and engaging can help businesses stay in the spotlight.
